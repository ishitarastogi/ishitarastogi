### About Me

- I'm a **Blockchain Research Analyst** focused on turning onchain data into actionable market intelligence.

- Currently building deeper expertise in **onchain data analytics, SQL, Python, and quantitative research**.

- My research interests include **Prediction Markets, DEXs, Stablecoins, Payments, and RWA**.

- I enjoy answering questions such as:
  - Who is actually driving market activity?
  - How does trader behavior change as probabilities move?
  - Where is liquidity flowing?
  - Which wallets consistently behave differently from the broader market?
  - What does onchain data reveal that headline metrics don't?

- Currently working with **Dune, SQL, Python, Pandas, and blockchain data** to build research-driven analytics projects.

- I write about **crypto markets, protocols, and onchain data**.

- My goal is to combine **blockchain research + data analytics + data science** to build better market intelligence.
